# Public content policy

Publish only source code, documentation, manuscript assets, synthetic demonstrations and the explicit aggregate results in this release. No raw or processed ADNI records, participant identifiers, individual predictions, subject-level partitions, checkpoints, credentials, fitted feature statistics or download archives belong here. `.gitignore` is a secondary guard, not a substitute for reviewing staged files.

The included CSVs contain one row per method or aggregated class-pattern cell. They are not participant-level datasets. The synthetic demo generates fictitious records in memory; its values are unrelated to ADNI participants.
