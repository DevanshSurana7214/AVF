# Mathematical and implementation audit

1. **Pattern:** `p=8*m_MRI+4*m_FDG+2*m_amyloid+m_tau`, with nonempty masks. Four modalities and three diagnoses define 45 nonempty pattern-by-class cells.
2. **Masked pooling:** four independent `d_k→64→64` MLPs; scalar gate scores for absent modalities are replaced by −1e9 before softmax. Weighted pooling also multiplies by the mask. A 64-value token and four availability bits form the 68-value head input; head layers are 68→64→3. Biases can make an absent branch's hidden vector nonzero, but its pooling contribution is zero.
3. **Dropout transition:** a singleton stays unchanged. A multi-modality pattern retains mass .5 and sends mass .5/k to each one-modality descendant. Each transition row sums to one; expected counts sum to training N. For 100 examples with MRI+FDG, masses are 50/25/25.
4. **Joint loss weights:** compute `sqrt(N/[45*(E+5)])`, clip to [.25,4], then divide by the expected example-weighted mean. The normalized weights need not lie inside the pre-normalization bounds. Do not normalize by a simple unweighted mean over cells. No-dropout weighting uses natural counts. The minibatch loss is the mean of weighted per-example cross-entropy.
5. **Fusion:** average probabilities over seeds before mixing. The archived fold alphas are .55/.60/.55. Convex mixing preserves nonnegativity and row sums. Every selected tau is zero, and `adjusted` returns the unaltered mixture at tau=0.
6. **Optional correction:** for positive tau the source uses `log(clip(p,1e-12,1)) + tau*(log(1/3)-log(clip(prior,1e-6,1)))`, then stable softmax. Priors use strength 20 and training class frequencies. Tuning averages priors of the other folds; application uses the target-fold training prior. The manuscript now states these details. This branch is not active in the reported AVF.
7. **Metrics:** macro-AUROC is the mean of three one-vs-rest AUROCs. Metrics use pooled fold predictions after seed averaging. SCR averages visit recall over a fixed set of 34 cells with at least 20 distinct participants, not 20 visits. Participant bootstrap intervals and original-sample point differences are distinct quantities.

The tests exercise expected-mass conservation, mean-one normalization, absent-branch invariance, singleton protection, training-only preprocessing, probability normalization, exact zero-correction bypass, subject-based support and frozen aggregate numbers. They check meaningful invariants; they do not certify scientific validity or independent generalization.
